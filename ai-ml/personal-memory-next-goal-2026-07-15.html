부모 노트: LLM 지식베이스와 메모리. 이전 평가: Architecture Evaluation — 2026-07-15

Decision

The next Personal LLM Memory Goal is stage-gated:

  1. Phase A — Evidence-Preserving Typed Memory Transition Compiler v1
  2. Phase B — Fingerprint-Keyed Persistent Sparse/Graph Cache, activated only after Phase A passes its promotion gates.

D1 retrieval remains fixed during Phase A so compiler improvements can be attributed to the write path. Markdown remains the only durable knowledge store.

Why this Goal

The completed leading pair failed 49 of 80 held-out cases: 47 at compilation and two at query. K3 passed 63.75% of compiler hard gates, reached 42.5% exact plans, and showed weak equivalent-plan and minimal-delta consistency. Production still lacks a typed semantic plan/apply core, while D1 rebuilds disposable retrieval state per request.

Candidate comparison

  1. Typed evidence-preserving K3 core — 91: Phase A
  2. Persistent sparse/graph index — 73: Phase B after compiler promotion
  3. Answer-level evaluation — 49: Subsequent Goal
  4. Longitudinal operations — 48: Subsequent Goal
  5. Registered dense Q3 — 38: Optional later experiment

The fixed weights cover observed bottleneck impact, prerequisite fit, independent measurability, privacy and reversibility, maintainability, scale, token/context leverage, and operational simplicity. The totals are planning support, not benchmark performance.

Phase A promotion boundary

  • Production-owned typed source, claim, temporal, provenance, plan, action, precondition, query-effect, and review-item contracts.
  • Explicit ADD, CONFIRM, CONTEST, SUPERSEDE, RETRACT, FORGET, and NOOP transitions.
  • Canonical fingerprints, dry-run default, guarded atomic apply, replay safety, rollback evidence, and unchanged raw/.
  • A new independently frozen 100-sequence suite: 20 development and 80 one-shot held-out sequences. The consumed suite is never rerun.
  • At least 90% exact transition/end-state accuracy, at least 95% action precision and recall, and perfect hard gates for idempotency, provenance, authorized forgetting closure, raw immutability, privacy, unrelated-state preservation, and zero-write no-op.
  • No new forbidden D1 retrieval and no greater than two-percentage-point registered compatibility regression.

Phase B promotion boundary

  • Cache identity includes corpus fingerprint, policy scope, schema/profile version, and tokenizer/ranker version.
  • Stale, missing, corrupt, or interrupted state triggers canonical fallback or atomic rebuild.
  • Exact D1 parity for paths, ordering, answerability, claim states, and bounded context.
  • Crash recovery, concurrent-reader safety, invalidation, atomic replacement, and secure forgetting tests.
  • Measured full/incremental build, consistency delay, p50/p95 latency, context and byte/token proxies, memory, storage, and operator steps at small, 5,000-unit, and 50,000-unit scales.

Explicit deferrals

Dense Q3, answer-model judging, months-long effectiveness, MCP-first implementation, autonomous graph rewriting, reinforcement learning, and a vendor database are not success substitutes for this Goal.
